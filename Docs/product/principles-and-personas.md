# Design Principles & Personas

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity** — Every screen communicates its purpose at a glance.
2. **Speed** — Optimise for performance and perceived speed.
3. **Trust** — Transparent feedback, privacy emphasis, safety by design.
4. **Delight** — Micro-interactions, animated feedback, friendly tone.
5. **Accessibility** — WCAG 2.2 AA.
6. **Scalability** — Components and tokens adapt as features expand.

---

## Personas

### 1. The Curious Duo (Friends)

_Lena, 25 (designer) & Amira, 26 (student)_

Friends in their mid-20s seeking spontaneous real-world experiences beyond nightlife or dating apps.

- **Goals:** discover local activities, expand social circle as a pair, easy weekend planning, safe new meetings
- **Pain points:** existing apps focus on dating or large groups; events feel impersonal; hard to match both friends' interests

### 2. The Adventurous Couple

_Marco, 31 (engineer) & Sophie, 29 (marketing)_

Couple wanting novelty through shared adventures with other couples — not seeking romance.

- **Goals:** meet couples with similar energy, find creative weekend ideas, host or join double-date experiences
- **Pain points:** hard to meet other couples organically; couple apps focus on parenting; meetups feel like too much effort

### 3. The Newcomer Pair (Expats / New Locals)

_Carlos, 27 (architect) & Diego, 28 (product designer)_

Friends who've moved to a new city wanting to build a social circle through authentic low-pressure experiences.

- **Goals:** meet locals naturally, explore the city, build a genuine social circle
- **Pain points:** cultural/language barriers; local events feel impersonal; fear of large or mismatched groups

### 4. The Parent & Child Duo

_Julia, 34 (mother) & Mia, 8 (daughter)_

Parent seeking family meetups where the **child is the main focus**, while also connecting with other parents.

- **Goals:** playdates where kids interact, connect with parents in similar life stages, outdoor activities for kids
- **Pain points:** family platforms lack parent-to-parent socialisation; hard to find age-matching kids; safety concerns with unfamiliar families

### 5. The Sibling / Twin Duo

_Anna, 24 & Lara, 24 (identical twins)_

Siblings or twins looking to meet **other sibling pairs** for duo-to-duo experiences.

- **Goals:** meet other twins/siblings, team-based events, community around sibling identity
- **Pain points:** very few opportunities for sibling pairs; mainstream events aren't designed for "pair identity"
